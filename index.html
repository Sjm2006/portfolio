WHO I AM

ABOUT ME

I'm a BCA student at Techno India, Salt Lake — working across the web stack with JavaScript, React, and Python. Over the past two years, I've shipped projects that range from a WebSocket-powered multi-user drawing tool (deployed on Railway) to a TypeScript health dashboard built under hackathon pressure.

My process is simple: pick a problem, start building, hit the walls, understand why they're there. That cycle is still how I learn best.

WHAT DRIVES ME

Mostly curiosity. I want to understand how things actually work — not just get them working. That's what pulls me toward API design, data structures, and thinking about state management even when the course doesn't require it.

I build tools I'd actually use. Clean interfaces, predictable logic, and UIs that don't make users think too hard — these aren't things I say to sound like a developer. They're just what I aim for every time I open a code editor.

A provably-fair on-chain coin flip dApp built with a Rust smart contract deployed to the Stellar Testnet via the Soroban platform.
